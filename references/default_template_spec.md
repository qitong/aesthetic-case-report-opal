# Default Template Specification (`aesthetic-case-report-opal`)

This document codifies the visual, structural, and clinical design system synthesized from the three reference pillars:
1. **Academic Literature Foundation**: *The Blending Technique: A New Approach for Tear Trough Correction* (Beut et al., *Aesthetic Plastic Surgery*, 2026).
2. **Academic Case & Literature Deep-Dive**: *Blending Technique 精读* (71-slide evidence-based anatomical and clinical appraisal deck).
3. **Editorial Clinical Case Report**: *Aesthetic Lives - Dr. Jovi Liu Edition* (Galderma GAIN 24-slide holistic assessment and longitudinal patient journey deck).

---

## 1. Visual Design System (Opal Medical Aesthetics Standard)

### 1.1 Slide Geometry & Grid
- **Slide Dimensions**: 16:9 Widescreen (`13.333 in` width × `7.5 in` height).
- **Safe Margins**: Left/Right `0.6 in`, Top `0.5 in`, Bottom `0.5 in`.
- **Header Zone**: Top `0.5 in` to `1.3 in` (Section tag in small caps + Action/Insight Title).
- **Content Zone**: `1.4 in` to `6.8 in` (Supports 2-column, 3-column, 5-column timeline, or Asymmetric Split layouts).
- **Footer Zone**: `6.9 in` to `7.3 in` (9pt muted citations, patient consent/confidentiality notice, and slide number).

### 1.2 Color Palettes

The default template provides two curated palettes depending on the presentation mode:

#### Palette A: Clinical Evidence & Anatomy (Inspired by *Blending Technique 精读*)
- **Background Primary**: Warm Alabaster `#F9F8F6`
- **Background Card / Surface**: Pure White `#FFFFFF` with subtle border `#E5E0D8`
- **Text Primary**: Deep Obsidian `#1A1D20`
- **Text Secondary / Captions**: Slate Gray `#5A626A`
- **Accent Primary (Clinical Slate)**: `#2C4C64`
- **Anatomical Semantic Colors**:
  - Ligaments (TTL / ORL): Cobalt Blue `#2B5B84`
  - Septum / Arcus Marginalis (AM) / Arterial Danger Zones: Crimson Red `#C0392B`
  - Gliding Spaces (Preseptal / Premaxillary / Prezygomatic): Emerald Sage `#27AE60`
  - Fat Compartments (DMCFP / SOOF): Amber Gold `#D4AC0D`

#### Palette B: Editorial Luxury & Patient Journey (Inspired by *Aesthetic Lives - GAIN*)
- **Background Primary**: Champagne Ivory `#FAF7F2`
- **Background Section Divider**: Deep Espresso Charcoal `#231F20`
- **Text Primary**: Charcoal `#231F20`
- **Text Secondary**: Warm Taupe `#7A7067`
- **Accent Primary (Opal Rose Gold)**: `#B8926A`
- **Accent Secondary (Soft Sand)**: `#E8DFD5`
- **Scale / Metric Bar Fill**: Muted Bronze `#9E7B56` (Active score) vs. Light Greige `#E2DBD3` (Inactive score)

### 1.3 Typography
- **Chinese Font Stack**: `PingFang SC`, `Source Han Sans SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`
- **English / Numeric Font Stack**: `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `Calibri`
- **Type Scale**:
  - **Section Divider Number**: 54pt Bold (Accent color)
  - **Slide Title (H1)**: 24pt–28pt Bold (Single-line core clinical takeaway)
  - **Subtitle / Deck Context**: 13pt–14pt Regular (`#5A626A`)
  - **Card Header (H2)**: 16pt–18pt Bold
  - **Body Copy / Clinical Notes**: 12pt–14pt Regular (Line spacing `1.3x`)
  - **Table Cells**: 11pt–12pt Regular
  - **Footer Citations & Photo Labels**: 9pt–10pt Regular

## 2. Core Slide Archetypes & Layout Blueprints

When using the default template, select and sequence from the following 10 canonical slide archetypes based on the user's input:

### Archetype 1: Editorial Cover Slide
- **Layout**: Full-bleed or clean minimalist canvas with top-left institutional/clinic tag, prominent bilingual title, subtitle indicating the anatomical region or technique, and clinician/author metadata at the bottom.

### Archetype 2: Section Divider (`01`–`07`)
- **Layout**: Minimalist transition slide with a large two-digit index (`01`, `02`), Chinese section title, and English subtitle (e.g., `03 解剖与分型 / Anatomy & Classification`, `05 治疗方案与技术 / Treatment Plan & Technique`).

### Archetype 3: Consensus & Mechanism Matrix Table
- **Use Case**: Summarizing literature background, mainstream clinical consensus, or complication mechanisms.
- **Columns**: `要素 (Element)` | `共识做法 / 本例方案 (Approach)` | `解剖与流变学依据 (Anatomical & Rheological Rationale)`.
- **Rows**: Sequence (顺序), Plane (层次), Dosage (剂量), Product Rheology (产品特性), Instrument (器械), Danger Zones (危险区).

### Archetype 4: Anatomical Cross-Section & Classification Split
- **Use Case**: Explaining patient classification (e.g., `Innie` vs. `Outie` based on Arcus Marginalis and ORL gap, or bone-to-dermis aging layers).
- **Layout**: Left 55% dedicated to annotated anatomical cross-section or 3D Vectra imaging; Right 45% dedicated to structured bullet points (`定义 / Definition`, `临床表现 / Clinical Presentation`, `注射策略 / Injection Strategy`).

### Archetype 5: Pre-Treatment Multi-Axis Facial Assessment Scale
- **Use Case**: Quantifying patient baseline status (inspired by Galderma Facial Assessment Scale & Beut-Jelks 8-Step Checklist).
- **Layout**:
  - **Left Column**: Patient baseline clinical photographs (Frontal & 45° Oblique).
  - **Right Column**: 5-domain visual severity bars (`0 = None`, `1 = Mild`, `2 = Moderate`, `3 = Severe`):
    1. **Expression (表情纹)**: Static lines (静态纹), Dynamic lines (动态纹)
    2. **Symmetry (对称性)**: Left/Right structural asymmetry
    3. **Proportions / Vector (比例与向量)**: Anterior midcheek negative vector, lid-cheek distance
    4. **Skin Quality (肤质)**: Loss of radiance/glow, loss of firmness/laxity
    5. **Volume Loss & Sagging (容量缺失与下垂)**: Deep fat pad atrophy (DMCFP), ligamentous tethering (TTL/ORL)

### Archetype 6: Holistic Treatment Plan & Rheological Mapping
- **Use Case**: Detailing the exact injection protocol, entry points, and bilateral volume breakdown.
- **Layout**:
  - **Center/Left**: Annotated facial diagram or baseline photo showing entry points (e.g., alar-tragus line × mid-pupil line intersection), cannula vectors (deep vertical parallel passes vs. trans-ligamentous comet passes).
  - **Right**: Product & Dosage Cards grouped by anatomical step:
    - **Step 1: Deep Structural Support (深层支撑)** — Target (e.g., DMCFP / Maxilla / Zygomatic Arch), Product & Rheology (high $G'$, high cohesivity), Instrument (e.g., 22G 50mm blunt cannula), Volume (`Left: X ml / Right: Y ml`).
    - **Step 2: Superficial / Space Blending (浅层/间隙过渡)** — Target (e.g., Preseptal & Premaxillary/Prezygomatic spaces), Product & Rheology (low $G'$, low water affinity), Instrument (e.g., 25G 50mm blunt cannula), Volume (`Left: X ml / Right: Y ml`).

### Archetype 7: Decision Algorithm / Flowchart
- **Use Case**: Visualizing clinical decision trees (e.g., Upgaze + Smile evaluation -> Innie vs. Outie -> Anterior Midcheek Negative Vector Yes/No -> Step 1 + Step 2 vs. Step 1 Only).

### Archetype 8: Standardized Before & After Comparison
- **Use Case**: Presenting immediate or follow-up clinical outcomes.
- **Layout Rules**:
  - Side-by-side equal-dimension image frames (`Before` on Left, `After` on Right).
  - Explicit view badge in top corner (`正位 Frontal` | `斜位 45° Oblique` | `侧位 90° Profile`) and expression badge (`静态 Static` vs. `微笑 Dynamic`).
  - Bottom callout bar summarizing total volume injected, follow-up interval, and GAIS score.

### Archetype 9: Longitudinal Patient Journey (3 to 5 Timepoints)
- **Use Case**: Tracking a patient across months or years (e.g., `Baseline 2024` -> `Post-Tx 1` -> `2025 Follow-up` -> `2025 Touch-up` -> `2026 Current`).
- **Layout**: 3 to 5 equal-width vertical photo columns aligned horizontally across the slide, with a connected timeline axis underneath showing the date/timepoint and key intervention or tissue state at each milestone.

### Archetype 10: Evidence Appraisal & Clinical Pearls Summary
- **Use Case**: Concluding with PICO evaluation, methodological limitations, or actionable clinical pearls (e.g., 3 numbered takeaway cards).

## 3. Custom `design.md` Override Protocol

If the user supplies a `design.md` file instead of choosing the built-in default template:
1. Parse `design.md` for custom slide dimensions, color hex codes, font families, slide sequence, and layout constraints.
2. Retain the clinical data integrity rules (strict photo aspect ratio preservation, bilateral L/R dosage clarity, anatomical layer specificity) while applying the visual and structural directives from `design.md`.

## 4. Clinical Photo De-identification Standard

Every patient photograph that enters a shareable case deck must have the orbital region obscured. The band is applied programmatically by `scripts/apply_eye_mask.py` **before** slide construction (SKILL.md Step 4).

| Item | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| Band geometry | Full image width; upper edge at `33%` and lower edge at `49%` of image height (`--top 0.33 --bottom 0.49`) |
| Fill | Solid, fully opaque Deep Obsidian `#1E2022` (matches Palette A text primary `#1A1D20`; use Charcoal `#231F20` for Palette B) |
| Edges | Hard edges, zero corner radius, both ends bleed off-frame. No feathering, blur, gradient or transparency — soft edges leak the orbital contour |
| Coverage target | Brows, upper and lower lids, and nasal root. On standardized frontal and 45° oblique portraits the eyes sit around `40–44%` of image height |
| Must remain visible | The chief-complaint anatomy (e.g. horizontal neck lines from ~`70%` down), the jawline, and the photo's standardized lighting/background |
| Special views | Lateral 90°, pediatric, or tight crops: inspect one image and override `--top` / `--bottom`; verify on one frontal and one oblique image before batch processing |
| Originals | Untouched copies kept in `<photo_dir>/_unmasked/` (or an explicit `--backup-dir`), never referenced by the PPTX build |
| Naming | Keep original filenames with `--in-place`, or add `_masked` suffix when writing to `--output-dir` |
| Deck annotation | Footer text `眼部已脱敏 / Eyes masked`; also recorded in the case-record markdown and structured data |

### 4.1 Rationale

The periocular region is a biometric identifier. In medical aesthetics decks, photographs are frequently copied into talks, shared folders and publications, so de-identification must be a build-time step rather than an afterthought. AI-generated/simulated portraits are treated identically: they represent "the patient" in the narrative and should follow the same privacy convention.

### 4.2 Verification Checklist

1. Open one masked frontal and one masked oblique image: no eyelid, lash line, brow tail or iris is visible.
2. Confirm the neck / target anatomy is unobstructed and the band is perfectly horizontal and full-width.
3. Confirm unmasked backups exist and are **not** in the image paths consumed by the PPTX builder.
4. Render the deck and confirm the footer states the de-identification status.
